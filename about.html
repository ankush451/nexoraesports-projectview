Our Story

Pioneering the Future of Esports Production

Founded in 2018, Nexora (Registration #EX-2025-001) began with a simple vision: to bring the same level of professional broadcast quality found in traditional sports to the burgeoning world of esports.

Today, we are a leading production house based in India with over 50 full-time professionals, ranging from broadcast engineers to creative directors, all united by a passion for competitive gaming.
